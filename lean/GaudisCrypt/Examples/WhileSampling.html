    Sampling until a test fails terminates (EasyCrypt examples/WhileSampling.ec) #

    Draw from a lossless distribution until a test fails; if the test fails with positive probability, the loop terminates with probability 1. EC's file, line by line; its one lemma, Sample_lossless, is proven with EC's script.

    Translation:

    • type t. op sample … axiom sample_ll … op test … axiom pr_ntest are the class Params (the file is its own theory).
    • EC's while I V k ε is phoare_while I V k ε (Logic/PHoareWhileRev.lean): the loop terminates because the integer variant V, at most k and positive while the test passes, decreases in each round with probability at least ε.

    Gap list:

    • GAP 1: EC's => // after seq and while closes a goal that is a hypothesis (ih, the axiom pr_ntest); here exact ih, exact pr_ntest.
    • GAP 2 (goals as shown): the body goal of phoare_while has EC's abstract statement w (the loop to come) as a variable of type Stmt; a judgment about w prints as a term (phoareStmt … w …), not as phoare[ … ].
