    Indifferentiability (EasyCrypt proof/Indifferentiability.eca) — EC-fidelity port #

    A construction C of a functionality from a primitive is indifferentiable from an ideal functionality F when some simulator S makes Indif(C(P), P, D) and Indif(F, S(F), D) indistinguishable to every distinguisher D.

    The theory's types are outParams of the (field-less) class Params p f_in f_out: a clone is clone Indifferentiability as Indiff0 with type p <- … (Syntax/Clone.lean), and inside the theory nothing is written. EC's subtyping of module types (FUNCTIONALITY <: DFUNCTIONALITY) is module_subtype. EC's call restrictions ({ P.f }) are not part of a module type (exception E2 of the port): they are the ext of declare module's representability.
