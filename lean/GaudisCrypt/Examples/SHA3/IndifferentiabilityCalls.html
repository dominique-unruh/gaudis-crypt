    Abstract calls of a simulator #

    EasyCrypt's call (_ : inv) on a simulator's procedure S(F).init, S(F).f, S(F).fi (in proc (inv) of an outer abstract call): from SIMULATOR.RepresentedBy, each is a procedure with the hole F.f (none for init), and the abstract call rule applies at a region F ⊇ glob S (prhlProc_abstract_callF). Used for the nested calls Dist(…, S(F)) of the indifferentiability proofs.
