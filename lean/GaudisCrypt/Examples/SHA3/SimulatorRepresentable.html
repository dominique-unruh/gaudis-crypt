    Simulators are representable #

    A SIMULATOR is a functor from DFUNCTIONALITY (one procedure) to PRIMITIVE (three). Each of the result's procedures f, fi, as a functor of the oracle, is a first-order functor module (SIMULATOR.fF, SIMULATOR.fiF: the simulator followed by a projection), and so is represented (Module.exists_representation_of_firstOrder). SIMULATOR.RepresentedBy also asks init to be a fixed procedure, independent of the oracle (EasyCrypt's init calls nothing): that is the one hypothesis of SIMULATOR.representable_of_init. Removes exception E2 for a concrete simulator.
