    Indifferentiability of the sponge (EasyCrypt proof/SHA3Indiff.ec) — EC-fidelity port, complete #

    The lemmas of SHA3Indiff.ec in EC's scripts, over the old port's declarations of Simulator, Cntr, FC, PC, DRestr (section FirstPort below), as SmartCounter/Gconcl.lean over the old Gconcl.

    Translation:

    • require import Common Sponge. import BIRO. is the open below.
    • EC's section declare module Dist <: DISTINGUISHER {-…} and declare axiom Dist_lossless are a declare module and a variable; the restriction also names the modules of the files whose lemmas the proofs use (fresh in EC). Dist has a region for its glob (with glob R, exception E14): security applies Sponge.conclusion and Gconcl_list.Real_Ideal to DRestr(Dist) and LowerDist(Dist), and Dist_lossless restricts its oracles against R.

    Declarations of the first port #

    Definitions and plain lemmas of the first port of this file (2026-10/11) that the proofs below use; its program-logic proofs are gone.

    The first port's translation notes (some mention declarations that went with its proofs):

    • require import Common Sponge. import BIRO. is the open below: unqualified names are Sponge's (DISTINGUISHER, RealIndif, LowerDist …) and IRO is Sponge.BIRO.IRO.
    • type state = SLCommon.state, op parse = BlockSponge.parse, op valid = Gconcl_list.valid and op limit = SLCommon.max_size are abbreviations.
    • Simulator and FC, PC have no type in EasyCrypt; they are used as a SIMULATOR, a FUNCTIONALITY and a PRIMITIVE, and declared at those types.
    • The section's declare module Dist and its restrictions are hypotheses (DistRestrictions); declare axiom Dist_lossless is the hypothesis Dist_lossless of the lemmas whose proofs use it (security).
