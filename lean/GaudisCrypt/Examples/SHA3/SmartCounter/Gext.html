    The extension event (EasyCrypt proof/smart_counter/Gext.eca) — EC-fidelity port #

    Real_G2 bounds the real game by the eager G2 plus the switching bound and the probabilities of collisions (Pr_G1col) and extensions (Pr_ext, through the resampling game Gext). Over the old port's declarations of G2, Eager, RRO (section FirstPort below), as SmartCounter/Handle.lean and SmartCounter/Gcol.lean.

    Translation:

    • the local modules ReSample, Gext are declared here as in EC, ReSample's variable count a global_var (ReSample.count); Gext.resample, Gext.M, Gext.S are modules of their own (EC's inner modules), Gext D the game with D a Lean parameter;
    • declare module D <: DISTINGUISHER {-C, -PF, -G1, -G2, -Perm, -ROhandle.RO.m, -Redo, -ReSample.count}: EC's freshness of the local module for D in D's restriction.
