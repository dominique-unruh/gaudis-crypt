    The collision event of G1 (EasyCrypt proof/smart_counter/Gcol.eca) — EC-fidelity port #

    Pr_G1col bounds the probability of G1.bcol by max_size · (2 max_size / 2^c), through the local game Gcol that counts the capacity samples. Over the old port's Handle declarations (as SmartCounter/Handle.lean).

    Translation:

    • the local module Gcol is declared here as in EC, its variable count a global_var (Gcol.count); its procedures Gcol.sample_c, Gcol.M, Gcol.S are modules of their own (EC's inner modules), Gcol D the game with D a Lean parameter;
    • declare module D <: DISTINGUISHER {-C, -PF, -G1, -Gcol.sample_c}: EC's freshness of the local module for D in D's restriction (as SecureRO's local modules).

    Declarations of the first port #

    Definitions and plain lemmas of the first port of this file (2026-10/11) that the proofs below use; its program-logic proofs are gone.

    The first port's translation notes (some mention declarations that went with its proofs):

    • clone export Handle as Handle0 clones a theory without parameters: it is Handle, opened.
    • The local module Gcol calls sample_c from its inner modules M, S: these are the modules Gcol.sample_c, Gcol.M, Gcol.S, and Gcol D the game (with D a Lean parameter).
    • The local module Gcol's variable count is the lens parameter Gcol_count of the local declarations (Gcol.sample_c, Gcol.M, Gcol.S, Gcol, Pr_col, G1_col), fresh by hypothesis; Pr_G1col does not mention it (exception E6).
    • EasyCrypt's 2^c (an int power, c > 0) is 2 ^ c.toNat; card (an int) is the cardinality cast to ℤ.
