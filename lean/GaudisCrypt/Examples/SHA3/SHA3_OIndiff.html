    EC-fidelity port

    SHA3OIndiff, the one lemma of SHA3_OIndiff.ec, in EC's script, over the first port's declarations of the file's modules (section FirstPort below: FSome, PSome, Poget, CSome, OSimulator, Counter, OFC, OPC, ODRestr, DFSome, DPSome, OD), as SHA3Indiff.lean over the first port's.

    Translation:

    • the lemma quantifies its own Dist (the section's declare module Dist is used by none of its lemmas): a declare module in a section of its own; the restriction also names the modules of the files security goes through (fresh in EC), and Dist has a region for its glob (with glob R, exception E14), since security is applied to OD(Dist);
    • EC's hypothesis on Dist has no restriction on F and P; security's has ({-OD(Dist)}).

    Gap list (batch-fix later, see TRANSFER_SKILL.md §1.6):

    • GAP 1 (closed): PSome(Perm).init behind the first port's record was not opened by inline*; it is since 2026-11-17 (procedures across clones' module types), and the hand unfolding left over from before went on 2026-11-21.
    • GAP 2: sim does not go through the loops of the inlined Sponge.f (GAP 2 of SHA3Indiff.lean): the while rule with the variables' equalities. (IRO.f's loop, whose relation after it reads x0{1} against x1{2}, which the loops keep: EC's sim since 2026-11-21, its framed loop rule.)
    • GAP 3: EC's sim n m and sp at the start of the two games are positions in EC's inlined code; ours has the inliner's reset_vars statements: wp for the last assignments, and the initializations stay before the call (EC's auto after it takes them).
    • GAP 4: the first port's OD(D, F, P) passes the oracles on as records of their procedures: the hypothesis on Dist is applied at those records (as GAP 6 of SHA3Indiff.lean).
