    A call of LoopSnoc.sample is a sampling of dlist #

    EasyCrypt's Sample_LoopSnoc_eq (DList.ec's Program theory), at the level of statements: the call x <@ LoopSnoc.sample(n) has the runs of the sampling x <$ dlist d n (LoopSnoc.call_denotation). prhlStmt_seq_dlist_left is EasyCrypt's transitivity{1} { x <@ LoopSnoc.sample(n); } (true ==> ={x}) (…) on a leading sampling: it replaces it by the call, whose loop can then be inlined and related to another loop. Kept apart from DList.lean so that its importers need not be rebuilt.
