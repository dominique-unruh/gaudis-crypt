    Diffie–Hellman assumptions (EasyCrypt theories/crypto/DiffieHellman.ec) #

    The theories DDH and CDH over a cyclic group of prime order (Lib/EC/Group.lean: EC's clone CyclicGroup as G, clone G.PowZMod as GP, clone GP.FDistr as FD at the head of the file). List_CDH and Set_CDH with their reductions are not ported yet.
