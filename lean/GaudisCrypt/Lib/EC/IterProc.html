    EasyCrypt's iteration of a procedure over a list (theories/looping/IterProc.eca) #

    The abstract type t is a section variable, so a clone is an application. Only what ported developments use is here.

    Iter(O).iter l is denotationally the calls of O.f on the elements of l, in order (Iter.iter_denotation, through iterDen). From that:

    • iter_ll and iter_inv are EC's, as stated there;
    • iter_cat, iter_perm and iter1_perm are EC's equivs with ={glob O} as denotational equalities (exception E9): stronger, valid for any O, and used by rewriting. EC's Iter(O).iters(l₁, l₂) and Iter(O).iter_1s(x, l) are spelled as the composite denotations they stand for, and EC's iter2_eq (iter_12 ~ iter_21) is the commutation of two calls of O.f.
