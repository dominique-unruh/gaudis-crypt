    EasyCrypt's list library, on int (theories/datatypes/List.ec) #

    EasyCrypt's list operations take and return ints (size, nth, take, drop, nseq, index, find, mkseq). To keep EasyCrypt statements as they are, they are mirrored here on ℤ, with EasyCrypt's behaviour on negative arguments: a negative count is 0. The operations that EasyCrypt shares with Lean (map, rev, flatten, ++, ∈) are Lean's.

    Only what ported developments use is here. Names are EasyCrypt's.
