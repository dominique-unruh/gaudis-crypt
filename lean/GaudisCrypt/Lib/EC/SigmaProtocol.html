    Sigma protocols (EasyCrypt theories/crypto/SigmaProtocol.ec) #

    EC's theory SigmaProtocol: the module types of a scheme and of its algorithms (extractor and simulator), and the experiments for completeness, special soundness and special honest-verifier zero knowledge.

    The theory's types are outParams of the class Params, its operators de and R the fields: a clone is clone import GaudisCrypt.EC.SigmaProtocol as SP with type statement <- …, op R <- …, op de <- …. EC's op slen, op wlen with their axioms slen_pos, wlen_pos are used by no declaration of the theory and are left out (EC's clones leave them abstract). R x w is a proposition; where a program stores it (r <- R x w) it is decided classically, as EC's logic is. EC's variable to is a Lean keyword, hence «to».

    SimulateHonestVerifier.main calls the module's own procedures (b' <@ gameIdeal()): written with their paths, SimulateHonestVerifier(S, A, D).gameIdeal.
