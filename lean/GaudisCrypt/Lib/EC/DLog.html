    The discrete logarithm assumption (EasyCrypt theories/crypto/DLog.ec) #

    The theory DLog over a cyclic group of prime order (Lib/EC/Group.lean: EC's clone Group.CyclicGroup as G, clone G.PowZMod as GP, clone GP.FDistr as FD at the head of the file): the standard experiment, the one whose adversary may fail, and the reduction of the second to the first (StdRedAdversary, dlog_standard_reduction).
