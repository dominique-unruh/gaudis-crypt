    EasyCrypt's fixed-length bit words (theories/datatypes/BitWord.eca) and bit chunking #

    (theories/datatypes/BitEncoding.ec, BitChunking)

    BitWord is an abstract theory over a length n : int with 0 < n. A clone fixes n and renames word, typically to block or capacity. Here the theory is the structure word n, and a clone is a namespace of abbreviations under the new names; see GaudisCrypt/Examples/SHA3/Common.lean.
