    Bit words: the algebra of +^ (EasyCrypt theories/datatypes/BitWord.eca) #

    The lemmas xorw0, xorwA, xorwC, xorwK of EC's BitWord, what its algebra uses on words (EC: clone import Ring.BoolRing as WRing), and the uniform distribution's facts that the conditions of rnd ask for. Names are EasyCrypt's.
