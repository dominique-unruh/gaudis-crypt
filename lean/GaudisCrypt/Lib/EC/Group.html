    Cyclic groups of prime order (EasyCrypt theories/algebra/Group.ec) #

    What a development gets by

    clone CyclicGroup as G.
axiom prime_p : IntDiv.prime G.order.
clone G.PowZMod as GP with lemma prime_order <- prime_p.
clone GP.FDistr as FD.

    a cyclic group group with generator g, its exponents exp (the field Z/pZ of PowZMod), exponentiation x ^ k, the discrete logarithm loge, and the uniform distribution dt on the exponents (FDistr). Only what ported developments use is here. Names are EasyCrypt's.

    The operations of the group and of the field are Mathlib's (CommGroup group, Field exp): EC's e, *, inv, / are 1, *, ⁻¹, /; the lemmas of EC's ZModpField on exp are those of a field (ring). The class Params group exp holds what Mathlib does not: the generator, the exponentiation by a field element, the logarithm and their laws. Its types are outParams, as for every theory: inside a development that clones it nothing is written.

    class GaudisCrypt.EC.CyclicGroup.Params (group exp : outParam Type) [CommGroup group] [Field exp] :
    Type

    EC's op g, op ( ^ ) : group -> exp -> group, op loge and their laws (expD, expM, expgK, loggK of PowZMod).

    • g : group
    • pow : group → exp → group
    • loge : group → exp
    • expD (x : group) (a b : exp) : pow x (a + b) = pow x a * pow x b
    • expM (x : group) (a b : exp) : pow x (a * b) = pow (pow x a) b
    • expgK (x : group) : pow g (loge x) = x
    • loggK (k : exp) : loge (pow g k) = k
