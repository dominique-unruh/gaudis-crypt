    Absolute values of advantages (EasyCrypt theories/crypto/AdvAbsVal.ec) #

    EC's module type Adv (one procedure main : unit -> bool) is a procedure module, procmod () → Bool; Neg_main(A) negates its answer. Neg_A_Pr_minus is how EC turns a bound on Pr[A.main() : res] into one on Pr[A.main() : !res] for a lossless A, and abs_val how a bound on the signed advantage gives one on its absolute value. EC's Pr[mu_not] is Pr_mu_not.
