    EasyCrypt's finite maps (theories/datatypes/FMap.ec) #

    ('a, 'b) fmap is a finite map, here Mathlib's Finmap. Only what ported developments use is here. Names are EasyCrypt's, with function names for its mixfix operators:

    m.[x]FMap.get m x
    m.[x <- y]FMap.set m x y
    x \in m (dom m x)FMap.dom m x
    rng m yFMap.rng m y
    fdom m, frng mFMap.fdom m, FMap.frng m
    restr f mFMap.restr f m
    find p mFMap.find p m
    map f mFMap.map f m
    noflags mFMap.noflags m
    m1 + m2FMap.add m1 m2
    in_dom_with m x fFMap.in_dom_with m x f
