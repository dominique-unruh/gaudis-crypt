    EasyCrypt's distributions (theories/distributions/) #

    EasyCrypt's 'a distr is a sub-probability, here SubProbability. Only what ported developments use is here. Names are EasyCrypt's.

              noncomputable def GaudisCrypt.EC.dexcepted {α : Type} (d : SubProbability α) (P : α → Prop) :
              SubProbability α

              d \ P: d conditioned on not satisfying P. EasyCrypt normalizes the restriction of d to ¬ P. Here that is done for lossless d with a ¬ P outcome of positive probability; otherwise the result is the null distribution, as in EasyCrypt when no such outcome exists.
