    Instantiate: shared base #

    RO procedure setup (RO_lazy/RO_eager, denotation bridges), the zoom/transferBy-agnostic monad-morphism lemmas, and the Footprint confinement core (ConfinedP, fvP_stmt, confinedP_of_fv, the Lens.lift framework, convertL_inFootprint). Shared by the transfer (TransferInstantiate) and relational (ROCouplingEquiv) developments.
