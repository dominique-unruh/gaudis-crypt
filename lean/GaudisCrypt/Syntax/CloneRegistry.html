    The registry of EasyCrypt clones #

    The clones clone (Syntax/Clone.lean) has made, and how a name of a clone's theory reads in the clone: what the framework needs to know of clones (declare module's restrictions and the footprints of concrete modules find a clone's variables under the clone's names; module paths follow a clone's aliases to the theory's modules). Apart from the command, so that the command can change without the framework being rebuilt.
