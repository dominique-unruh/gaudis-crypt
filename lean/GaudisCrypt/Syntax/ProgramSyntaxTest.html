                                  Printing and round-tripping #

                                  #roundtrip t prints t with the delaborators of ProgramSyntax.lean, parses the printed text again and checks that what comes back is definitionally equal to t. The printed text is logged and pinned by the #guard_msgs docstrings — a delaborator that quietly gave up (so that Lean printed the raw constructor term) would still round-trip, but would not print the surface syntax, and the docstring catches that.
