    EasyCrypt's call (_: ={glob A, x₁, …}) on an abstract procedure #

    equiv_call (_ : ={glob A, Bounder.bounder, Counter.c, RO.m})

    The last statements of both sides call the same declared module A (declare module), with oracles O₁, O₂ (A(O₁).f, A(O₂).f). EC's call (_: ={glob A, X}): the two calls agree on their results and on glob A and X afterwards, if their arguments and glob A, X agree before, and if the oracles' procedures keep that agreement (prhlProc_abstract_call_module, the invariant globInvF X (glob A) Eq).

    Goals, as EC's: the oracles' judgments (oracle, one per procedure of the oracles' module type, each equiv[ O₁.f ~ O₂.f : ={arg, glob A, X} ==> ={res, glob A, X} ]), then the code before the calls (pre). The representation of A comes from its declaration and the oracles' module type T.ext; A's disjointness from X and the oracles' countable arguments are discharged as for hoare_call (: inv). glob A may be written or left out: it is always part of the invariant.

    On a call A(O₁) ~ A(O₂) of a declared module, Logic/ECCallUpto.lean's rule for the same syntax comes first: EC's oracle judgments, without glob A (the oracles' writes keep it). The rule here is the fallback, and the rule for another declared module's glob in the set.
