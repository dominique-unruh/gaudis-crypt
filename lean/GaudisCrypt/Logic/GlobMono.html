    glob is monotone #

    EasyCrypt's glob (Footprint.glob, the slots of the variables a footprint touches) grows with the footprint: a variable touched by F ≤ F' is touched by F'. A region G within glob GF then stays within glob (GF ⊔ X), which is how a region hypothesis (exception E14 in notes/SHA3/Exceptions.md) is extended by further variables. A global_var is finitely supported, and outside glob F when outside F (for F finitely supported).
