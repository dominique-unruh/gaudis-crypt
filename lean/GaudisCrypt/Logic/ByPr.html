    EasyCrypt's bypr, on phoare judgments #

    bypr is the converse of byphoare (Logic/PrTactics.lean): a procedure judgment about an event,

    phoare[ p : P ==> E ] ≤ (ENNReal.ofReal x)        (or `=`, `≥`)

    its bound a real (as byphoare and phoare_call (: P ==> Q) leave it: EC's bounds are reals), becomes the statement about the probabilities of the runs that satisfy the precondition,

    ∀ a &m, P a m → Pr[ p (a) @ &m : E ] ≤ x

    (EC's goal after bypr=> &m: the arguments and the memory are introduced by intro).
