    pHL on an exact bound: EC's rnd E, conseq, islossless #

    EC proves phoare[ c; x <$ d : P ==> Q ] = r by

    rnd E.                      (* c : P ==> mu d E = r /\ forall v, v \in d => (E v <=> Q v) *)
conseq (: _ ==> true).      (* the condition holds in every state *)
islossless.                 (* c is lossless *)

    The judgments of Logic/PHoare.lean are about expectations, and their rules for a sampling (phoare_auto, rnd_pred1) work on the expectation it leaves, for upper bounds. Here are EC's three steps for an exact bound, on the judgment itself.
