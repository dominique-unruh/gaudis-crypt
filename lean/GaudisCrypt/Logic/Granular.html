    Granular regions: EasyCrypt's glob M as a lens region #

    EasyCrypt's memory is a set of whole variables, and glob M is the set of variables M touches. Here the grains of the variable granularity (variableGranularity) are the variables' slots, and the granular cover of a module's footprint (Footprint.IsSubGranular.granularCover) is its glob: the slots it touches, whole. A cover is a lens region (GranularFootprint.footprint_fromLens), so a run that stays out of it leaves its content unchanged (ProgramDenotation.preserves_touched_of_fromLens) — which footprint-disjointness alone does not give (CounterExamples/FramingNeedsReset.lean). An EasyCrypt-like module has one (Footprint.FinitelySupported.isSubGranular), and a restriction against a variable carries over to the cover (Footprint.IsSubGranular.granularCover_le_compl). Evaluation: notes/SHA3/GlobGranularity.md.
