    Chains of lens writes in normal form #

    lensWriteNorm (a simproc): two writes of the same lens are one, two writes of disjoint lenses in a fixed order. Used by sim's implications (Logic/ECSim.lean) and by stmt_swap's side goals (Logic/StmtSwap.lean): two chains of writes on the same state are equal once in normal form exactly when they write the same values to the same lenses.
