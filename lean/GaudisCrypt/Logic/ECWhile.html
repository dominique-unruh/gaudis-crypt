    EasyCrypt's while I V on a phoare … = 1 judgment #

    phoare_while I V        -- EC's `while I V`: the loop runs out with probability 1

    On a goal phoare[ … ==> E ] = 1 { s; while c body } (EC proves a loop lossless, or that it ends in E, with an invariant I and a variant V): the body, from I, the test and V = k, ends in I with V < k with probability 1 (body, for every k); the code before the loop ends in I with probability 1. The side conditions — E holds on leaving the loop from I (exit), the expectation is at most 1 (bound) — are closed when simp can.

    EC's while (true) (size p - i): I is True, the variant size p - i (here a natural number, (size p - i).toNat).
