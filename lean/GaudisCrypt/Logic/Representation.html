    Representing functor modules by procedures with holes #

    The abstract call rule needs an adversary D as code: a procedure with holes p that D, applied to oracles O, behaves like when filled with ext O (Module.RepresentedBy, Logic/AbstractCall.lean). Exception E2 of the SHA-3 port takes this as a hypothesis. Every module is code, though: a closed normal module expression. This file works towards extracting the representation from that normal form, for functors over first-order oracle types (products of procedures), with the full list of the oracle's procedures as ext.

    • Substituting procedures with holes for holes (StmtWithHoles.substHoles, ProcedureWithHoles.substHoles): each hole call becomes the flattened call of the substituted procedure, everything else is re-targeted along Flatten.trafo. Sound (ProcedureWithHoles.procedureDenotation_substHoles), from the inliner's term-level lemmas (StmtWithHoles.equivInLens_flattenCall, StmtWithHoles.applyLens, the EquivInLens congruences). This is the extraction step for a procedure with holes applied to a tuple.
    • First-order normal forms (ModuleExpression.FONormal, foNormal_of_hasType, Module.expression_shape): over first-order parameters a normal term of first-order type is a procedure, a tuple, a component of a parameter, or a procedure with holes applied to such a tuple. These are the cases the extraction recurses on.
    • The footprint of a substitution (FVP.fvP_proc_substHoles_le): no more than the procedure's own and the substituted procedures'. fvP sees a procedure's globals only (Lens.reduceFootprint ProgramState.globalL), and the renamings of the locals are invisible there: a kernel on the globals commutes with zoom l p once it commutes with p, for l keeping the globals (Footprint.GlobalsCommute.zoom, by Lens.reduceFootprint_alt_def). The callee frame's reset and parameter writes are on the locals alone (footprint_zoom_localL_le).
    • The pieces of the extraction: instantiations from families (HoleSigs.Instantiation.ofFun), congruence of instantiation in the denotations (ProcedureWithHoles.procedureDenotation_instantiate_congr), components of a literal oracle (ModuleExpression.FOProj.reduce_substitute), the wrapper of a hole (ProcedureWithHoles.ofHole, which needs nonempty parameter and result types: they live in local slots) and closed procedures in a hole context (ProcedureWithHoles.ofProc).
    • The extraction (FONormal.extract, by induction over first-order normal forms) and its module form, Module.exists_representation: every first-order functor module is represented by a procedure with holes over the full list of the oracle's procedures, with at most the footprint of its own normal form.
    • Module types (Module.exists_representation_of_firstOrder, Module.representable_of_firstOrder): a moduletype is a tree of products without the trailing unit. Its procedures in order are T.holesApp .empty, a literal module's are T.extOf O; ModuleExpression.rebuild rebuilds the module from the tuple (ModuleTypeRep.rebuild_extOfApp), which reduces a functor over T to one over the tuple, at no cost in footprint. So Module.Representable D ext holds for every functor module D over a first-order module type whose ext is the full list of its procedures: exception E2 is a theorem there.
