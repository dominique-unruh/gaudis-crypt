    EasyCrypt's inline specifications: call (: P ==> Q), byphoare (: P ==> _) #

    EC states the judgment about a callee, or the one a probability is read from, where it is used:

    phoare_call (: P ==> Q)          -- EC's `call (: P ==> Q)` in a `phoare`
phoare_call (: P ==> Q) ≤ r      -- … with the callee's own bound
hoare_call (: P ==> Q)           -- EC's `call (: P ==> Q)` in a `hoare`
byphoare (: P ==> _)             -- EC's `byphoare (: P ==> _)`

    P and Q are written as in phoare[ M : P ==> Q ] (Syntax/PHoareSyntax.lean): P reads the callee's parameters and the globals, Q its result res and the globals. The judgment about the callee becomes the first goal, tagged spec (EC's order); the code before the call is the second. In a phoare the callee's relation is the goal's, and its bound the goal's, which must then not depend on the state, or the one written after the parenthesis.

    hoare_call (: inv) (Logic/FelTactic.lean, an invariant of an abstract module's oracles) is the form without ==>.
