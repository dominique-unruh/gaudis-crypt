    EC's lossless distributions, as a simp set #

    @[ec_lossless] theorem bdistr_expected_one : bdistr.expected (fun _ => 1) = 1

    EasyCrypt's auto takes a sampling x <$ d of a lossless distribution (is_lossless d, dunifin_ll, …) as one that happens: what rnd leaves of it, the mass mu d predT, is 1. Ours is the expectation d.expected (fun _ => 1); the facts d.expected (fun _ => 1) = 1 of the distributions a development uses are tagged @[ec_lossless], and the auto tactics (equiv_auto, phoare_auto, the strict ec_auto) and the closers' ec_prep simplify with them.
