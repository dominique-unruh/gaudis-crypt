    equiv_call (_ : P ==> Q)    -- EC's `call (: P ==> Q)`: the callees' judgment, a new goal
equiv_exfalso               -- EC's `exfalso`: the precondition is contradictory

    EC's call (: P ==> Q) on the last statements x₁ <@ M₁(e₁) and x₂ <@ M₂(e₂) of the two sides asks for the judgment equiv[ M₁ ~ M₂ : P ==> Q ] (first goal), and before the calls for P at the arguments and, for every results and globals Q relates, the postcondition — where what the callees do not touch keeps its value (EC's memories past a call). That frame is found as EC finds it: the variables and glob regions the postcondition reads that a callee stays out of (fp_disjoint, Module.Proc.frame_lens, Module.Proc.frame_glob); the rule is prhlStmt_seq_call_frame.
