    pHL loops that terminate with probability 1: EC's while I V k ε #

    EC's rule for phoare[ while b c : P ==> Q ] = r (ecPhlWhile.ml, t_bdhoare_while_rev_geq): an invariant I, an integer variant V bounded by k on I and positive where the test passes, and a probability ε > 0 with which one round decreases the variant. Then the loop terminates with probability 1 from I (Stmt.ewp_while_one_of_variant), and its expectation is the one solution of the loop's equation (phoareStmt_while_rev_eq).

    The zero-one argument of Stmt.ewp_while_one (Logic/PHoare.lean) is the case I = True, k = 1: there, one round leaves the loop with probability ε.

      EC's while I V k ε on phoare[ while b c : P ==> g ] = bd (ecPhlWhile.ml, t_bdhoare_while_rev_geq), EC's six conditions in its order:

      • hpre: the precondition gives the invariant;
      • hbound: where the loop is not entered, the bound is the postcondition;
      • hterm: on the invariant the variant is at most k, and positive where the test passes;
      • hbody: one round followed by any statement w that meets the judgment meets it (EC's abstract statement standing for the loop);
      • hinv: one round keeps the invariant, with probability 1;
      • hε0, hvar: one round decreases the variant with probability at least ε > 0.

      Beyond EC's: g ≤ 1 and bd ≤ 1 (an event and a probability), and some state satisfies the postcondition (hsat). The last is needed here because w is a quantified statement, not EC's abstract one: if no state satisfies the postcondition, no w meets the judgment with a bound other than 0, and hbody holds for want of one — while (b) b <- false from b with the postcondition false would pass the six conditions for any bound. (In EC the judgment about w is a hypothesis that can only be used, not refuted.)
