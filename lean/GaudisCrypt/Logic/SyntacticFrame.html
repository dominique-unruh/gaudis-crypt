    The syntactic frame: what a statement does not write, it keeps #

    EasyCrypt frames a call's invariant syntactically. An oracle that writes none of D's variables keeps glob D, and the oracle premise of the abstract call rule then needs only the oracle's own relation (={arg} ∧ inv ==> ={res} ∧ inv). prhlProc_globInvF_of_confined does this from a footprint (inFootprint), which a concrete procedure does not come with.

    The syntactic route instead looks at every write of the statement, its callees' bodies included: StmtWithHoles.WritesKeep G s says each of them keeps G of the globals. A write to a local variable keeps the globals outright. A write to a global lens outside a region F keeps F.touched_getter (touched_set_of_le_compl). hoareStmt_of_writesKeep turns that into a Hoare triple by induction over the statement. prhlProc_globInvF_of_writesKeep puts it into the oracle premise.

    The tactic writes_keep reduces a WritesKeep goal to the conditions on single writes. It unfolds module procedures as the inliner does (Flatten.unfoldProcedure), closes the writes to local variables by rfl, and the writes to global lenses outside the region by touched_set_of_le_compl with a hypothesis from the context.
