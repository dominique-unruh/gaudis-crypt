    A closer for what EC leaves to auto => /# #

    auto_close

    EC closes the logical goals its program tactics leave with smt (/#, smt(…)). We have no SMT solver (by decision), but those goals come in a few shapes, and auto_close takes them apart the way one does by hand:

    • introduce everything, take the hypotheses apart (∧, ∃, ∨), substitute the equations of variables (the states of EC's sp: σ = x.set v τ);
    • simplify the reads through writes everywhere, the hypotheses included (simp_reads_all; through the inliner's renamings, rename_reads), and the glob of a region past writes outside it (glob_simp, EC's implicit ={glob D});
    • the tests back to propositions (decide … = true), indicators of events (indicatorE P = 1);
    • split the goal (∧, if), and close each part by rfl, an assumption (either way round), trivial, omega (EC's /# on counters), or simp_all.

    It closes the goal or fails, leaving it untouched. It does not know the lemmas EC's smt(l) is given: state those first (have := l …).

        def GaudisCrypt.tacticSubst_frames :
        Lean.ParserDescr

        EC's memories as one: an equation between reads of the same variable in two states (the frame X.get τ = X.get σ that conseq and call leave for what the code does not modify, or ={x}) rewrites the read of the later state (in the context) into the earlier one's, everywhere. One direction for every pair of states, so the rewriting cannot loop; of two equations saying the same (X.get τ₁ = X.get τ₂ from an invariant, X.get τ₂ = X.get τ₁ from a frame) one is dropped, so that simp_all does not loop on them either.

                                                    def GaudisCrypt.«tacticSmt_close[_]» :
                                                    Lean.ParserDescr

                                                    EC's smt(l₁, …): auto_close's preprocessing, the reads of the states as variables (generalize_reads_all), their equations substituted, then grind with the lemmas lᵢ; if that fails, once more with the lemmas' instances at the goal's terms as hypotheses (ec_instances: EC's provers instantiate them there, grind's E-matching may not, through definitions).

                                                    Then, what EC's provers also do:

                                                    • the predicates of the file being checked that the goal mentions are unfolded (EC's pred: its body is known to the provers; a predicate of the library is named, smt_close [P]);
                                                    • linear arithmetic over an ordered field (linarith: grind does not do it over the reals);
                                                    • the lemmas are instantiated in a chain (ec_instances_chain: at the terms of the instances too, their hypotheses taken from the context), for EC's smt(l₁ l₂ l₃) on a chain of (in)equalities between probabilities.

                                                    A parameter that is not a name — a lemma applied to its arguments, smt_close [l A m] — is a fact: it becomes a hypothesis first. So does the name of a lemma with no argument but instances (smt_close [dbool_ll, gt0_n]).
