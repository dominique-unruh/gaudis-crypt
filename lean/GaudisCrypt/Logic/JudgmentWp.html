    Backwards wp steps, for every registered judgment #

    Judgment.wpStep k processes the last k statements of a judgment's statement one at a time, from the end:

    • deterministic code (Judgment.detOf?: assignments, resets, skip, sequences and conditionals of those) by the judgment's detLemma — the postcondition after the state update, the same shape in every judgment;
    • a sampling by the judgment's sampleLemma, a call by its callLemma, a conditional with calls or samplings in its branches by its ifLemma — where judgments genuinely differ;

    simplifying the postcondition after each step with the judgment's postSimp. hoare_wp, phoare_wp and the dispatching stmt_wp are this one step.
