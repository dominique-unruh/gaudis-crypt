    Golden tests of equiv_rnd f g #

    The goal EC's rnd f g leaves: its three conditions (see Logic/ECAutoTest.lean for the format; scripts/update_golden.py refreshes the expected text).

    The module declared by module L: the record of its procedures L.f.

    The declaration has no parameter list, so no procedure of it can call one either and the module is not a function — it is the plain record itself, and there is no L.apply_simp to apply it with.
