    One assignment per parameter of an inlined call #

    EasyCrypt inlines f(e₁, e₂) as x₁ <- e₁; x₂ <- e₂; …, one assignment per parameter; our inliner writes x₁, x₂ <- (e₁, e₂). EC's positions after an inline then count more statements than ours, and a swap that moves a statement between two parameters has nothing to move past.

    stmt_split_params turns every such tuple assignment into EC's sequence (Stmt.split_pair): the tuple assignments that follow a reset_vars (what inline leaves in front of the parameters). Written after stmt_inline* until the inliner does it itself.
