    pHL on exact bounds: seq, case, conseq #

    The rules EasyCrypt's scripts use on phoare[ c : P ==> Q ] = r when r is not 1 (PIR.ec's Pr_PIR_s, vonNeumann.eca):

    • seq n : E p₁ p₂ p₃ p₄ I (phoareStmt_seq_eq): c₁ reaches E with probability exactly p₁ and ¬E with p₃; from E (and the invariant I) c₂ reaches the postcondition with exactly p₂, from ¬E with p₄; and p₁ p₂ + p₃ p₄ = r. A bound _ leaves its goal out: EC allows it where the other bound of the branch is 0. On a lower bound (phoareStmt_seq_ge): the same with ≥, and r ≤ p₁ p₂ + p₃ p₄.
    • case E (phoareStmt_case).
    • conseq (: _ ==> _ : = e): another bound, equal under the precondition (phoareStmt_conseq_bound).
    • conseq (: P ==> _): a weaker precondition (phoareStmt_conseq_pre).
    • conseq (: _ ==> Q₁) (: _ ==> Q₂): the event Q₁ in pHL and Q₂ in Hoare logic, where under Q₂ the two events Q₁ and Q agree (phoareStmt_conseq_hoare).
    • conseq H for a judgment H about the same statement (phoareStmt_conseq_of).

    The tactics are in Logic/PHoareExactTactics.lean.
