    EasyCrypt's byequiv and byphoare, on probabilities #

    A goal about probabilities, as Pr[ M (a) @ &m : E ] writes them (Syntax/PrSyntax.lean: the real (Procedure.Pr p a m E : ℝ)), is turned into a judgment:

    • byequiv — Pr[ p₁ … ] = Pr[ p₂ … ] (or ≤, or EC's up to bad Pr[ p₁ : E₁ ] ≤ Pr[ p₂ : E₂ ] + Pr[ p₂ : F₂ ], Q giving E₂ ∨ F₂) from equiv[ p₁ ~ p₂ : P ==> Q ], with the precondition holding of the two runs' arguments and memories and Q relating the events (E₁ ↔ E₂, for ≤ E₁ → E₂). Without a term, the judgment is the new goal with EC's defaults: P is ={arg, glob} (the arguments and the globals agree) when both sides run on the same arguments from the same memory, else arg{i} = aᵢ ∧ glob{i} = mᵢ; Q relates the events. With a term h (a judgment), the side conditions are the goals.
    • byphoare — Pr[ p … ] ≤ x (or =, ≥) from phoare[ p : P ==> E ] ≤ r (=, ≥), the bound r read at the run's arguments and memory against ENNReal.ofReal x (EC's bounds are reals). Without a term, the judgment is the new goal, with the precondition arg = a ∧ glob = m and the bound ENNReal.ofReal x.

    The side conditions left are tried by rfl/trivial/simp_all; 0 ≤ x (a bound below a probability is non-negative) by positivity.
