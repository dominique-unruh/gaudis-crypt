    pHL on exact bounds: the tactics #

    The rules of Logic/PHoareExact.lean as EasyCrypt writes them, on a goal phoare[ c : P ==> Q ] = r:

    • phoare_seq n : E p₁ p₂ p₃ p₄ I — EC's seq n : E p₁ p₂ p₃ p₄ I (I may be left out: EC's default true, its goal closed). The bounds are reals; _ leaves a bound open where the other bound of its branch is 0 (its goal is not asked). On a lower bound ≥ r: the same goals with ≥, _ a bound 0, and r ≤ p₁ p₂ + p₃ p₄. Goals in EC's order: invariant, first_E, second_E, first_notE, second_notE, bound (the reals' equation when the judgment's bound is ENNReal.ofReal e), and nonneg if 0 ≤ pᵢ is not found.
    • phoare_case (E) — EC's case (E): goals pos, neg.
    • phoare_conseq (: _ ==> _ : = e) — another bound e (a real): goals bound, then the judgment.
    • phoare_conseq (: P ==> _) — a weaker precondition: goals pre, then the judgment.
    • phoare_conseq (: _ ==> Q₁) (: _ ==> Q₂) — goals post (under Q₂, Q₁ iff the judgment's event), hoare (the triple for Q₂), then the judgment for Q₁: EC's order.
    • phoare_conseq H — from a judgment H about the same statement: goals pre, bound (closed when trivial).

    Tests: Logic/PHoareExactTest.lean.
