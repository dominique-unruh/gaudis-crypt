    EasyCrypt's transitivity{1} with a reflexive first leg #

    transitivity{1} { c₂ } (E ==> F) (P ==> Q) replaces the left program c₁ by c₂: a judgment c₁ ~ c₂ : E ==> F, with E reflexive (EC: its forall &1 &2, P => exists &m, E /\ P side goal, discharged by taking &m = &1), and the judgment c₂ ~ c₃ : P ==> Q' where Q' asks Q of every left state that F relates to the middle one. prhlStmt.trans_left is that instance of prhlStmt.trans; it fixes the middle judgment's conditions, so that it can be left as a goal.
