    The syntactic frame for expectations: concrete supermartingales #

    EasyCrypt's fel bounds a bad event through the oracles: each call increases the potential (the bad event plus what remains to be paid) by at most nothing in expectation. For a concrete oracle that changes the potential's state only through a few sampling procedures (Gcol.sample_c), the step lemma of those procedures is all that matters: every other write keeps the state.

    StmtWithHoles.ExpDecr G φ s says that of s: each write keeps G of the globals, and each call either is to a procedure that does not increase φ ∘ G in expectation or has a body of which ExpDecr holds again. Stmt.ewp_le_of_expDecr concludes that s does not increase φ ∘ G (loops with φ ∘ G as the invariant). The tactic exp_decr reduces an ExpDecr goal to the single writes as writes_keep does, closing a call by a step lemma from the context when one applies.
