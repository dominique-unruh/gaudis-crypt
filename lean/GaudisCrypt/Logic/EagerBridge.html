    From relational statements to the eager judgment #

    ProgramDenotation.eagerR S S (=) p q (=) (Logic/EagerRhl.lean) is a judgment about two composite programs on the globals, S; p and q; S. EasyCrypt proves its eager lemmas by the relational tactics on those composites. This file lets the equiv_* tactics do the same.

    • ProgramDenotation.preserves_compl_of_inFootprint: a run confined to a lens X keeps everything outside X (X.compl).
    • ProgramDenotation.eagerR_of_prhl2_lens: the frame. When S, p and q are confined to X, a coupling of the composites with equal results and equal X is the full equality-invariant eager judgment. EasyCrypt's eager lemmas about an oracle state X (={x, FRO.m} ==> ={res, FRO.m}) are proven at this postcondition.
    • prhl2_composite_of_prhlStmt: the composites are the runs of the statements _ <- R(); r <- p(a) and r <- q(a); _ <- R(), so a prhlStmt between those statements gives the coupling.
    • eagerR_of_prhlStmt: the two together.
