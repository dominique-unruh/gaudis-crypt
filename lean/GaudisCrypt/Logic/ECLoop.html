    EasyCrypt's splitwhile and unroll #

    stmt_splitwhile{i} n : e     -- EC's `splitwhile{i} n : e`
stmt_unroll{i} n             -- EC's `unroll{i} n`

    The loop at EC's position n (from 1, the resets inlining leaves not counted, as for stmt_swap [a..b] k) is rewritten by an equivalence of statements: while c b becomes while (c /\ e) b; while c b (splitwhile), or if c { b; while c b } (unroll). On any registered judgment, on side i of a relational one. e is written like a condition of the goal (§x reads the program variable x) and may be a proposition (decided) or a boolean.

    After splitwhile the two loops are two statements (the sequence is re-associated), so EC's positions after them count as in EC.

    splitwhile holds for every loop, as in EC (Stmt.Equiv.splitwhile'): the split loop's expectation is the original's by the least-fixed-point property of loops alone (Stmt.ewp_while_le), no variant needed.
