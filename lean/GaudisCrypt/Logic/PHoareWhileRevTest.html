    Golden tests of phoare_while I V k ε, and of conseq, rnd on a lower bound #

    The goals EC's while I V k ε leaves on a loop that flips a coin until it shows heads (see Logic/ECAutoTest.lean for the format; scripts/update_golden.py refreshes the expected text).

    The body of proc run of module Flip, as a procedure of its own: it calls no module parameter, so it has no holes.

    A hole stands for a call whose callee is not known here — a module parameter is supplied only when the module is applied — so the body is a ProcedureWithHoles, to be instantiated (see Flip.run.procedure.apply_simp) rather than run. Use Flip.run for this procedure as a module.
