    pHL's seq with five bounds (EasyCrypt's seq n : E p₁ p₂ p₃ p₄ I) #

    For an upper bound on c₁; c₂, EC's

    seq n : E p₁ p₂ p₃ p₄ I

    splits at the n-th statement over the intermediate event E: c₁ reaches E with probability at most p₁ and ¬E with at most p₃; from E (and the invariant I, which c₁ establishes) c₂ reaches the postcondition with at most p₂, from ¬E with at most p₄; and p₁ p₂ + p₃ p₄ ≤ r.

    phoareStmt_seq_le is the rule; phoare_seq n : E p₁ p₂ p₃ p₄ I applies it, splitting after the first n statements (EC's count), with E and I written like the judgment's conditions (§x). The goals, in EC's order: I (a Hoare triple about c₁), c₁'s bounds for E, c₂'s from E ∧ I, c₁'s for ¬E, c₂'s from ¬E ∧ I, the arithmetic. A bound 1 on an event (EC's 1%r) is closed on the spot.

    seq n : P on a lossless goal #

    For phoare[ c₁; c₂ : A ==> 1 ] = 1 (islossless), EC's seq n : P asks that c₁ reaches P with probability 1 and that c₂ is lossless from P (phoareStmt_seq_eq_one): phoare_seq_lossless n : (P), goals first, second. With P = True (EC's seq n : true) the first goal is the losslessness of c₁.
