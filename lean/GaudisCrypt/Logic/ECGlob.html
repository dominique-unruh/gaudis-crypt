    EasyCrypt's glob M #

    equiv[ CF(DRestr(D)).main ~ G1(DRestr(D)).main : ={glob D} ==> … ]

    glob M is the part of the globals the module M touches: the getter R.touched_getter of a region R (glob_region M), whose value at two global states agrees exactly when they agree on R (Language/Footprint.lean). A getter is read like a variable, so ={glob D} in a relational condition is §(glob D){1} = §(glob D){2}, EC's ={glob D}.

    The region is EC's:

    • a concrete module's glob is its variables (EC's vars of the module, here the global_vars C.x its procedures use): their footprints joined, C.c.footprint ⊔ C.queries.footprint for module C with C.c, C.queries (the region of their tuple Lens.pair C.c C.queries); the closers read ={glob C} as ={C.c, C.queries} (TacticMisc.globTuples);
    • any other module (a declared one, an application) has the footprint FVP.fvP M.

    glob is a keyword where this file is imported: the call tactics (equiv_call (_ : ={glob A, …}), the frames / glob A) read it in this form.
