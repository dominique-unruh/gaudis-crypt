    EasyCrypt's fel (the failure event lemma), once #

    EC's

    fel init cntr step q bad [O₁: pre₁, …] inv

    bounds the probability of a failure event bad after an abstract module's run by ∑_{i ∈ [init, q)} step i: each oracle call that may set bad (its preᵢ holds) does so with probability at most step cntr and increases the counter cntr; the other calls leave bad and cntr alone; inv holds throughout.

    Here the same premises, for the oracles of Module.app D O with D abstract, give the bound, by Module.ewp_app_le (the supermartingale abstract call) on one potential, felPotential, once for all games: ⊤ off the invariant, 1 once bad holds, else the bound still to pay, ∑_{i ∈ [cntr, q)} step i. The state the premises talk about is the view L.get g of the globals through a lens L that D does not touch (the counter, the variables of bad and inv).

    Deviation from EC: bad must persist (FelOracle.keep), where EC's conclusion is about bad at the end however it got there; the events of the SHA-3 games are monotone. The premises are Hoare and pHL judgments about the oracle procedures, proven with the usual tactics.
