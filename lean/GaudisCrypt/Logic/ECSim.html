    EasyCrypt's inline* and sim #

    stmt_inline*     -- EC's `inline*`: every call whose callee is spelled out, on both sides
stmt_inline*{i}  -- … on side `i`
phoare_inline*, hoare_inline*
sim              -- EC's `sim`, on a relational judgment about two statements

    inline* inlines call sites until only calls of abstract procedures are left (EC's inline*, which does not inline a declared module either).

    sim goes backwards over the two statements, computing the relation needed before each step from the one needed after it, as EC's sim does with its sets of equal variables, but with any relation: deterministic code on either side by wp (stmt_wp: copies, dead assignments and the frame resets of inlined calls disappear into the relation), samplings on both sides coupled by the identity (equiv_rnd), calls of the same procedure on both sides from equal arguments and globals (equiv_call), conditionals on both sides (prhlStmt_seq_if_bwd): equal guards, each branch's relation found by sim on the branches (a branch it does not finish stops it at the conditional), and loops on both sides (prhlStmt_seq_while): EC's equality set as the invariant (the relation after the loops and the variables they mention equal), the bodies by sim. Where both statements are used up, the precondition must imply what is left (equiv_skip), closed by simp_all if it can; anything else (calls of different procedures, a sampling against other code) stops sim with that goal.
