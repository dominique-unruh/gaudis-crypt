    Programs commuting with a state update, through loops #

    ProgramDenotation.CommutesU p u: running p from u st is running it from st and applying u to the final state. It is preserved by ⊥, pure, bind and the suprema of chains, and so by while_loop (the Kleene argument of ProgramDenotation.EquivInLens.while_loop). This is what stmt_swap and stmt_alias need of a loop (Stmt.CommutesWith.while, Logic/StmtSwap.lean): a loop whose guard u leaves alone and whose body commutes with u commutes with u.
