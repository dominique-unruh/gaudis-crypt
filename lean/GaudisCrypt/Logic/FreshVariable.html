    Fresh variables #

    EasyCrypt gives a local module or a local clone declared in a section new global variables, disjoint from those of every module the section declares. Here such variables are lens parameters of the local declarations, with freshness hypotheses (exception E6 of the SHA-3 port), and a non-local result instantiates them with lenses that are actually fresh.

    Fresh lenses need not exist for an arbitrary footprint: a statement's getters are arbitrary functions of the state, so a module may read, say, every ℤ-typed slot, and then no ℤ-typed variable is fresh for it. EasyCrypt's modules have finitely many global variables. This file states that as Footprint.FinitelySupported: the footprint lies within the slots of finitely many variable names. Under it, Footprint.exists_fresh gives a fresh variable of any nonempty type, itself finitely supported, so that several fresh variables can be chosen one after the other.
