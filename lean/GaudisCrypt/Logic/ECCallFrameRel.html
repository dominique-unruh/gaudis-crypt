    The relational call rule with the callees' frames #

    prhlStmt_seq_call_frame: EC's call keeps what the callees do not touch. The postcondition is asked for the results and globals the callees' judgment relates and that are in each side's frame of its globals before the call (Module.Proc.Frame: a variable outside the callee's footprint keeps its value, Module.Proc.frame_lens). ECCallEquiv.frameFor builds the frame of a callee for the variables a condition reads.

    Used by the call tactics for concrete callees (Logic/ECCallEquiv.lean) and for the procedures of a declared module with several procedures (Logic/ECCallAbstract.lean).

    source

    M.Frame fun (g g' : VariableAssignment) => X.get g' = X.get g

    EC's frame by writes: a lens that every write of M's body keeps (its callees' included) is a frame of M — a callee that reads X without writing it keeps it, where Module.Proc.frame_lens asks M to stay out of X altogether.
