    Footprints of concrete modules, read off their code #

    EasyCrypt reads a concrete module's globals off its code: no lemma states what Bounder or D1(A) touches. Here the same, for the footprint solver:

    • fp_disjoint (this file's elaborator, extending Logic/FootprintSolver.lean's): a concrete module M with no @[fp_bound] bound is bounded by the globals its code mentions (DeclareModule.globalVarsOf, through the modules and definitions it uses) and the abstract modules among its Lean arguments, FVP.fvP M ≤ FVP.fvP A ⊔ (Lens.pair x₁ (… xₙ)).footprint, proven by fp_module;
    • fp_finite: F.FinitelySupported for a join of module footprints and variables, the modules declared (Module.Declared, Module.ECModule) or concrete (bounded as above);
    • ec_module: Module.ECModule D ext for a concrete D over a first-order module type, its representability Module.representable_of_firstOrder, its finite support fp_finite.
