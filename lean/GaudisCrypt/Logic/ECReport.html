    Reporting what a tactic did not do #

    A tactic of the EC layer that cannot do a step as specified and continues another way (a weaker rule, a goal left in another shape) says so: ECReport.fallback. By default this is a warning, so the proof still goes through and the build lists every place where it happens; with

    set_option ec.strict true

    it is an error. What is too frequent for a warning goes to the trace classes ec.frame (what a frame keeps and drops), ec.rule (which rule or form a tactic chose), ec.close (the closers' steps): set_option trace.ec true shows all of them.

    Failures that are part of a search (the next candidate is tried) are not reported.
