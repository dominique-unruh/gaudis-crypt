    EasyCrypt's bypr, on equiv judgments #

    EC's bypr (res{1}) (res{2}) proves that two procedures return the same result,

    equiv[ p₁ ~ p₂ : P ==> ={res} ]

    from the equality of the probabilities of each result,

    ∀ a₁ &1 a₂ &2 v, P → Pr[ p₁ (a₁) @ &1 : res = v ] = Pr[ p₂ (a₂) @ &2 : res = v ]

    (prhlProc_res_of_Pr_eq). The coupling is not built by hand: two programs whose results have the same distribution are related by the wp-lifting for the relation "the same result" (ProgramDenotation.rel_fst_of_wp_eq), and the wp-lifting gives a coupling (discrete Strassen: ProgramDenotation.relE.to_prhl2).
