    States equal except on a lens #

    Lens.equal_outside L g₁ g₂ (Language/Lens.lean): g₂ is g₁ with L overwritten. The relation EasyCrypt's sim keeps when the two programs differ only in variables the rest of the code does not read (a local module's variable written on one side only, or written on both sides at different times). prhlProc_self_equal_outside is the call rule for it: a procedure that does not touch L, on both sides.
