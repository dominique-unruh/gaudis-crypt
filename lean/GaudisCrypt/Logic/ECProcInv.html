    equiv_proc (I) #

    On a judgment about the same abstract procedure with two oracles, EC's proc (I) keeps the invariant I across it if the oracles do. I is written as for equiv_call (_ : I) (Logic/ECCallUpto.lean): ={glob M, x, …} ∧ J or a relation J between the two memories.

    The abstract procedure is either

    • D(O₁).f ~ D(O₂).f with D declared (declare module): the rule of equiv_call (_ : I);

    • a procedure that a rule tagged @[proc_inv] is about: a procedure of a declared functor with several procedures (a simulator S(F).f), whose oracle lists declare module does not state. A rule has the form

      … → prhlProc (fun a₁ g₁ a₂ g₂ => a₁ = a₂ ∧ globInvF L F I g₁ g₂) p₁ p₂
      (fun r₁ g₁ r₂ g₂ => r₁ = r₂ ∧ globInvF L F I g₁ g₂)

      with arguments named L, F, I; its premises are closed from the context (a hypothesis about the module, assumption), by the footprint tactics, or are the oracles' judgments.

    Goals: the oracles' judgments, EC's ={arg} ∧ I ==> ={res} ∧ I when the oracles' writes keep the region (glob of the modules, read off the goal's precondition), else with the region in them; then pre, EC's two implications (the goal's precondition gives the invariant, the invariant the postcondition), as one statement about no code, unless ec_auto closes it.

    The same rules serve EC's call (_ : I) on such a procedure as the last statement: equiv_call (_ : ={glob M, …} ∧ I) (goals: the oracles' judgments, then the code before).

    equiv_conseq_frame H #

    equiv_conseq H (Logic/ECConseq.lean) asks H's postcondition to imply the goal's. EC's conseq H also knows what the procedures do not write: a variable or a glob the goal's postcondition reads and a procedure stays out of keeps its value. Goals pre (the goal's precondition implies H's) and post: for arguments and globals that satisfy the goal's precondition, results and final globals that satisfy H's postcondition and are in each side's frame satisfy the goal's; an implication that simplification closes is closed. The procedures of the goal are read off their module views first (module_norm), and a procedure named differently from H's is recognised by its body. The rule is prhlProc_conseq_frame.
