    The eager abstract-call rule for modules (EasyCrypt's eager proc on D(O)) #

    eager_call (Logic/EagerProc.lean) is EasyCrypt's eager proc for a procedure with holes: it needs the procedure's own steps to swap with the block (SwapLoc) and each hole call to be eager. Here the first condition comes from footprints, as EasyCrypt's comes from its syntactic check that the block and the adversary share no variables.

    • swapLoc_of_confinedP: steps confined to a region R swap with a block confined to a region outside R (transferBy_refl_of_inFootprint_compl, step by step).
    • inFootprint_zoom_globalL: a block on the globals confined to a lens X is, zoomed to procedure states, confined to globalL.chain X.
    • eager_call_module: EasyCrypt's eager proc for a module D : Module.Arr T (procmod …) that is representable over ext and disjoint from X, for a block S confined to X: each oracle procedure of ext O₁ eager against that of ext O₂ makes D(O₁) eager against D(O₂). At equality invariants; EasyCrypt's invariant I is added by a self-coupling (ProgramDenotation.eagerR_of_self_right).
