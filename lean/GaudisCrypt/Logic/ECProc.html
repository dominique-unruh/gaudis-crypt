    EasyCrypt's proc: a procedure judgment as the judgment about its body #

    phoare_proc     -- EC's `proc` on `phoare[ M.f : P ==> Q ] ⋈ r`
hoare_proc      -- EC's `proc` on `hoare[ M.f : P ==> Q ]`
equiv_proc      -- EC's `proc` on `equiv[ M₁.f ~ M₂.g : P ==> Q ]`

    The goal becomes the judgment about the procedure's body, its parameters local variables under their own names, the postcondition read at the return value: no call, no copies of the arguments, no reset of the callee's frame, so that EC's statement counts (seq 1, wp 2) are ours.

    Sound as EC's proc is: the body's judgment is proven from every state whose parameter variables hold the arguments, whatever the other locals hold (phoareProc_of_body_anyLocals); the run of the procedure starts from one of them (its other locals at their initial values). It is unprovable only for a body reading a local before writing it, as in EC.
