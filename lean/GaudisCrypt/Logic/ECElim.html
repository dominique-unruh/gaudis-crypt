    EasyCrypt's elim * on a judgment's precondition #

    equiv_elim* => a b      -- EC's `elim * => a b` (pRHL)
hoare_elim* => a        -- the same on a Hoare triple

    EC's sp over x <- e with e reading x leaves exists x0, P[x0/x] /\ x = e[x0/x] in the precondition; elim * takes the existentials out, as universally quantified names. Here: the existentials of the precondition moved outward over its conjunctions, then introduced one by one (prhlStmt_exists_pre of Logic/PRHLStmt.lean, hoareStmt_exists_pre), named as written (_ for an anonymous one). The names go as EC's: sp nests the right side's existentials outside the left side's, so a relational judgment's are named from the innermost.
