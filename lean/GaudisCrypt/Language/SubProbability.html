    Discrete subprobability monad #

    Discreteness invariant #

    A measure on the discrete (⊤) σ-algebra is discrete when it is the sum of its point masses, μ A = ∑_{x ∈ A} μ {x}. This is the invariant our semantics always satisfies (every measure is built from pure/bind/uniform/⊥), and it is exactly what lets the framework reconstruct a measure from its singletons and swap integration order without any countability assumption on the type — replacing the [Countable a] side-conditions (the goal of subtask 4).
