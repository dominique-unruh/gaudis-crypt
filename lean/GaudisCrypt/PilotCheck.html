    The day-to-day check: the tactic tests and the SHA-3 port #

    lake build GaudisCrypt.PilotCheck

    builds the tests of the tactic layer (Logic/*Test.lean, the golden tests among them) and the SHA-3 port (Examples/SHA3/, the EC-fidelity port: the former Examples/SHA3/Pilot/, hence the name), with what they import, and nothing else: about 70% of the project. Use it after a change of a tactic. The full lake build (every module) is for before a push; a file outside this check that an intended tactic change breaks is taken out of the barrel and retired, not repaired (2026-11-13).

    A new tactic test is added here and to GaudisCrypt.lean.
